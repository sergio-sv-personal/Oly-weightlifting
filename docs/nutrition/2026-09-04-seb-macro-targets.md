## The fat problem

Food4Fitness meals are lean across the whole range — 7–28g fat regardless of what the
description promises. The "Loaded Chicken Creamy Alfredo" is 9.7g; the laksa, at 28g,
is the only meal that hits target on its own. **Fat will not be solved by menu choice.**

Solve it at home instead, roughly 16g/day:

- **Half a medium avocado** ≈ 160 kcal / 15g fat / 7g fibre — preferred, since the
  calories and fibre are both wanted.
- **1 tbsp olive oil** ≈ 120 kcal / 14g fat — the reliable backstop for when there
  isn't a ripe avocado.
- **Not before training.** Fat slows gastric emptying; keep the pre-training feed to
  low-fat, low-fibre carbs.

## Pre-training, at 80g

Old norm was ~40g (pouch + banana). Combinations that reach 80:

- 2 pouches + banana + 2 corn thins ≈ 83g
- Half bagel with honey + banana + pouch ≈ 75g
- Slice of Turkish pide + banana + pouch ≈ 76g

Keep fat and fibre low in that feed. A banana is the cheapest 27g in the kitchen —
worth always having some in.

**Fibre ceiling, learned 11 Sep.** Three bananas + a pouch hit 93g of carbs with zero
fat, which looked ideal — but it also carried **10g of fibre**, and it caused GI
discomfort late in the session. Carbs were not the problem. **Cap the pre-feed at two
bananas** and take the balance from a bagel: bagel + banana + pouch delivers the same
83g on **3g of fibre**, and has run clean on 6, 8 and 9 Sep.

First attempt (Sat 5 Sep, 8:30am class, no banana in the house): apple + 2 pouches +
2 corn thins = **57g from food, 65g counting the coffee's oat milk.**

## 9 Sep — the weight data settles it

The RENPHO export through 9 Sep closed the gap that made the "is 2,846 enough"
question unanswerable. Over the 15 closed days:

- Mean intake **2,842 kcal/day** — within 4 kcal of target.
- Rolling-7 weight **80.83 → 79.59 kg** (−0.61 kg/week overall).

Split in half, though, it is two different stories:

| | Intake | Weight slope |
|---|---|---|
| 25 Aug – 1 Sep | 2,745 kcal/day | −1.07 kg/week |
| 2 – 8 Sep | 2,953 kcal/day | **+0.60 kg/week** |

208 kcal/day is worth about 0.19 kg/week, not 1.67 — so most of that swing is
glycogen and bound water, tracking the carb rise from 313 to 351 g/day. The early
drop was the same effect in reverse.

Interpolating for a flat trend puts break-even near **2,880 kcal/day**. Seb's number
is close to right, and the argument for raising it has evaporated: **the loss has
already stopped.** Hold at 2,846 and reassess after another fortnight with fewer
outlier days.
