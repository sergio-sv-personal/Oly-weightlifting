# Serving sizes — how much of each

Derived 10 Sep 2026 from Seb's 4 Sep macro split and the habits actually in the
ledger. This is the answer to Seb's own open question: rough serving sizes per
meal, rather than a meal plan.

Targets: **training day 2,860 / 160P / 375C / 80F**, **rest day 2,760 / 160P / 350C / 80F**.

## Step 1 — the habits already cover most of it

These run automatically and don't need deciding:

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Coffee at home | 75 | 1 | 9 | 4 |

| Night stack | 15 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| *Training days only:* bagel + banana + pouch | 413 | 10 | 83 | 3 |

| **Training day total** | **1,607** | **81** | **219** | **39** |

## Step 2 — what's left for the two real meals

| Per meal | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Training day** | **626** | **40** | **78** | **20** |
| **Rest day** | **848** | **44** | **122** | **22** |

Rest-day meals are *bigger*, which looks wrong until you remember the pre-training
feed and the Gatorade deliver 113g of carbs on training days. Take those away and
the carbs have to come from the plate.

## Step 3 — the plate

Build each meal from four slots. One item from each.

### Protein — aim 40g

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breast, cooked | **130g** |
| Chicken thigh, skinless | **160g** |
| Beef mince (5% fat), cooked | **150g** |
| Tuna in springwater, drained | **160g** — one and a half tins |
| Salmon, cooked | **160g** (also covers the whole fat slot) |
| Eggs | can't get there alone — 3 eggs is 18g, pair with something |

### Carbs — 78g training day / 122g rest day

| | Training | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| White rice, cooked | **280g** (~90g dry) | **435g** (~140g dry) |
| Pasta, cooked | **250g** (~100g dry) | **395g** (~150g dry) |
| Mashed potato | 520g — impractical, pair it with bread | |
| Bagel | 1¾ | 2¾ |

### Fat — aim 20g, and this is the one being missed

| | Fat |
|---|---|
| Half an avocado (100g) | 15g |
| Olive oil, 1 tbsp | 14g |
| Garlic aioli, 20ml | 13g |
| Feta, 30g | 6g |

**If the protein is fatty, it is the fat slot.** Salmon, pork belly, salami, chicken
with skin, regular mince — these carry 18–35g on their own. Don't then add avocado
or aioli. That double-count is the single reason fat has been running 100g+ instead
of 80.

### Veg — 150g

Two handfuls. Frozen mixed is fine and is what's usually in the freezer.

## Worked examples

**Training-day dinner** — 280g cooked rice, 130g chicken breast, 150g veg, half an
avocado → 634 / 46P / 86C / 20F.

**Rest-day lunch** — 395g cooked pasta, 150g 5% mince, tomato-basil sauce, 150g veg
→ 855 / 45P / 125C / 18F.

**With a Food4Fitness meal** — the meal averages 671 / 49P / 63C / 17F, so it covers
protein and fat on its own. Add **150g veg** and, on a rest day, **150g of cooked
rice** to close the carb gap. Don't add a fat source.

## The quick version, no scales

- Protein: a palm and a half of cooked meat, or 1½ tins of tuna
- Carbs: 1½ cupped handfuls of cooked rice on a training day, 2½ on a rest day
- Fat: one thumb — *unless the protein is already fatty, in which case none*
- Veg: two handfuls
