# Nutrition Session with Seb — 24 Aug 2026

Zoom call (distilled from a rough voice transcript). First nutrition consult, following
the cycle 2 check-in where Sergio asked for help transitioning from "eating to lose"
to "eating to perform."

## Seb's diagnosis

- Sergio has effectively been in a **deficit for ~a year** (since Oct 2025). Weight has
  plateaued at 80–81kg for ~6 months not because he's at maintenance, but because the
  body has **adapted to chronic under-eating** — holding on to what it gets.
- Current intake: **~1,800–2,000 kcal/day** — around what the body needs just for base
  function, before any training.
- Estimated maintenance for his size and training load: **~2,600–2,900 kcal/day**.
- Protein is high (~180–200g/day) — more than needed. Likely to be trimmed toward
  ~1.8 g/kg (~145g) to make room for carbohydrates.
- Carbs are the big gap: currently ~150–200g/day; a CrossFitter's eventual target is
  more like **~350g/day**.
- People telling Sergio he "keeps losing weight" while the scale holds = body likely
  scavenging; not a good sign, matches the yellow recovery scores.

## The plan (phase 1 — this week)

1. **Track food for 7 days** in an app — rough estimates are fine, photos okay,
   include weekends. Send the week's snapshot to Seb (WhatsApp) next Monday.
2. **Don't change the base diet yet** — Seb wants a true baseline. The ONE change to
   make now: **add carbs around training**:
   - **Pre-training** (30–45 min before): simple carbs, ~0.5–1 g/kg bodyweight
     (roughly 40–80g). Ideas: bagel + honey (no peanut butter pre-training — fat slows
     it down), rolled oats + protein, banana + dates for Altona evenings.
   - **Intra-training**: a carb drink — juice box, or Powerade powder in the water
     bottle — especially for classes and Saturdays. Also covers electrolytes.
   - **Post-training**: carbs + protein (not just the WPI shake).
3. **Vegetables**: basically absent from the diet — add a cup of frozen microwave veg
   with the evening meal. **Fruit**: banana counts; add one more with breakfast.
   Fibre goal ~30–35g/day (not tracked, just directionally more).
4. Keep weighing daily as usual; watch the trend, not the days.

## Phase 2 (after the tracked week)

- Seb reviews the baseline, adjusts in his spreadsheet ("add food here and there" —
  building on the current diet, not replacing it), sends changes.
- Work up toward the 2,600–2,900 range. **Goal: find true maintenance** — no target
  weight. Success = feeling energised, not gaining/losing uncontrolled.
- Sit at maintenance for a couple of months before any surplus conversation.
- Macro fine-tuning comes later; calories and training-window carbs first.
- Follow-up consult in ~1 month (Sergio's request — long enough to see a monthly
  weight trend through daily fluctuation).

## Watch-outs Seb named

- The body may need time to relearn using carbs around training — possible early GI
  awkwardness; benefits may not be instant.
- Sergio may not recognise "fuelled" — after a year of deficit, the reference point
  is gone. Expect the new normal to feel unfamiliar.
- The fear: "eating more = weight going back up." Reframed: this is an experiment to
  find maintenance, with daily weighing as the guardrail. If the trend moves wrong,
  calories get adjusted — controlled, strategic, nothing like the previous unmanaged
  regains.
